## 🎯 What is ToadStool?

**ToadStool** is a **universal compute platform** providing secure, multi-runtime execution environments for the ecoPrimals ecosystem.

### Core Capabilities

- **Multi-Runtime**: Native, WASM, Container, Python, GPU
- **Capability-Based Discovery**: Find services by capability, not hardcoded endpoints
- **Security-First**: Sandboxing, resource limits, policy enforcement
- **Ecosystem Integration**: Works with Songbird, BearDog, NestGate, Squirrel
- **Privacy-Preserving**: "Each primal knows only itself"
