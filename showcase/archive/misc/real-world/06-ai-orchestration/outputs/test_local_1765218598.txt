Spoiler Alert: This story contains spoilers for the movie "The Journey of Shadow" by Mitsuya Kondo and Yukio Tsuchiya, which is based on the novel of the same name by Satoru Tanaka.

In a futuristic society where artificial intelligence has advanced to a point where it can interact with humans on a personal level, there is a young woman named Ava who is a specialist in AI development. She receives an unexpected invitation from her boss, who wants her to join his team of AI scientists for a mission deep into the jungle to research a new species of organism that has evolved on a parallel planet. As she boards the helicopter and prepares to fly, she realizes that something is wrong with it - there's an unusual energy pulsing from it. Ava realizes too late that she has somehow misjudged her own abilities and the danger of what she's about to undertake.
