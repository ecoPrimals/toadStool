### **Technical Insights**:

1. **WGSL Alignment**: vec3 in uniforms needs [f32; 7] or [u32; 7] padding
2. **Workgroup Sizes**: 256 optimal for 1D, 16x16 for 2D
3. **Reduction Patterns**: Single workgroup for small reductions
4. **Binary Operations**: Standard 3-binding pattern
5. **Uniform Params**: Always bytemuck::Pod + Zeroable
