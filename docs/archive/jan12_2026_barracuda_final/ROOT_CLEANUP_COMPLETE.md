### What Stays at Root

✅ **Essential navigation** (README, START_HERE, STATUS, etc.)  
✅ **Major components** (barraCUDA mission & summary)  
✅ **Evolution reports** (dated, comprehensive, permanent value)  
✅ **Development guides** (TESTING, encryption, etc.)

### What Goes to Archive

❌ **Session summaries** (date-specific, one-time)  
❌ **Temporary status** (superseded by newer docs)  
❌ **One-time reports** (historical value only)  
❌ **Draft/interim docs** (superseded by finals)

### Principle

**Root = Permanent & Essential**  
**Archive = Historical & Session-Specific**
