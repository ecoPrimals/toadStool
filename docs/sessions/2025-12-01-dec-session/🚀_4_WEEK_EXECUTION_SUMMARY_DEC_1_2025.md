## 💡 KEY PRINCIPLES

### 1. True Concurrency ✅
- NO `#[serial]` (except extreme chaos)
- NO `sleep()` for coordination
- Use: `Notify`, `Barrier`, `channels`

### 2. Test Issues = Production Issues ⚠️
- Fix root causes, not symptoms
- Every test failure reveals a bug
- Concurrent-safe by design

### 3. Modern Idiomatic Rust 🦀
- Zero-copy where possible
- Proper lifetimes over clones
- `Cow<str>` for conditional ownership
- Event-driven coordination
