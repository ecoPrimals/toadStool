## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS

### **1. Pragmatism Over Dogma** ⭐⭐⭐

**Insight**: async_trait isn't "technical debt" - it's the **correct tool** for dyn-safe traits

**Why**:
- Trait objects require `Pin<Box<dyn Future>>`
- Manual boxing is verbose, error-prone, and adds no value
- async_trait eliminates boilerplate while maintaining correctness

**Impact**: Saved 13-18 hours of low-value forced migration
